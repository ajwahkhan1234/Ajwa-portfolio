AM

I am a full-stack developer who also works on SEO. I build sites and tools for law firms, clinics, e-commerce brands and agencies.

Most of my work starts with a problem rather than a brief: a site that won’t index, a store that needs to move platforms, a WordPress install that keeps getting reinfected, or a team still running on spreadsheets. I find out what is really happening, measure it, then build the smallest thing that fixes it properly.

I work in Next.js, React, WordPress and Elementor, Shopify, Medusa and Postgres, and I use local AI models where they help.

Belief

A good website stays fast, gets found in search and doesn’t need a developer every time someone wants to change a word.
